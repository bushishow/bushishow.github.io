Magician for Corporate Events in New York

Magic by Dash · Dashnor Bushi

A corporate event lives on its atmosphere. The moments guests remember are the ones that surprise them, and that is exactly what a professional magician brings to the room. Dashnor Bushi is a New York-based magician and mentalist who performs at corporate events across the city.

Why magic works at corporate events

Business guests are often reserved at the start of an evening. Close-up magic solves that instantly and gives people something to talk about for the rest of the night.

  • Breaks the ice during cocktails and networking
  • Keeps guests engaged between speeches
  • Creates shareable, memorable moments

What the show includes

Every event is tailored: strolling close-up magic during the reception, plus an optional stage set of mentalism and comedy for the main moment. Ideal for galas, product launches, trade shows and holiday parties.

How to book

Share your date, venue and audience, and you will receive a tailored proposal. Performances are available in English, Italian and Albanian.

Internationally acclaimed, available worldwide

Dashnor Bushi is a magician, mentalist and comedian, and a member of the Society of American Magicians. His son Nikolas is a ventriloquist, magician and singer who won Albania's Got Talent and was seen on Italy's Rai 1. Together the family has earned two Golden Buzzers and a following of millions. Based in New York, they perform solo or together for clients all over the world.
