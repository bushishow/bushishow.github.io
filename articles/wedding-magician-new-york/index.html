Magic by Dash · Dashnor Bushi

Weddings have beautiful but predictable gaps: the cocktail hour, the wait between courses, the lull before the dancing. A wedding magician fills those moments with wonder and keeps every guest smiling.

Magic during the moments that matter

Dashnor Bushi mingles with your guests during the reception, performing close-up magic table to table — the perfect way to entertain family and friends while the newlyweds are away for photos.

Why couples choose a magician

Elegant, never intrusive

The magic is tailored to the tone of your day: refined and charming, adding to the celebration without ever taking it over. Wedding dates book early — share your date and venue to check availability.

Internationally acclaimed, available worldwide

Dashnor Bushi is a member of the Society of American Magicians. Together with his son Nikolas — a ventriloquist and winner of Albania's Got Talent, seen on Italy's Rai 1 — the family has earned two Golden Buzzers and a following of millions. Based in New York, they perform for clients around the world.
