The Ventriloquist

Nikolas: three languages, one extraordinary voice.

Born in Ferrara, Italy, to Albanian parents and raised in New York, Nikolas Bushi grew up between three languages: English, Italian and Albanian. And he learned to make all of them come out of a puppet.

He started magic at age 4, inspired by his father, the magician Dashnor Bushi. Then he discovered ventriloquism and, with his puppets, built an audience of millions across TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

In 2026 he conquered Albania's Got Talent, from the Golden Buzzer to the trophy, and brought the house down on Italian national TV. This is only the beginning.

The Magician

Dashnor Bushi: where the magic began

Born in Albania, raised on stage in Italy and now based in New York: Dashnor Bushi blends stunning sleight-of-hand, mentalism and comedy. A sought-after performer for corporate and private events throughout New York City, a member of the Society of American Magicians, and the only magician ever invited to VentFest.

He is also the reason all of this exists: it was his magic, practised at home as a passion, that inspired 4-year-old Nikolas to pick up his first trick.

In 2026, father and son each earned a Golden Buzzer at Albania's Got Talent and reached the final together. One family, two acts, one unforgettable show.
