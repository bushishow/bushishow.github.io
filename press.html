Nikolas Bushi, ventriloquist

Winner of Albania's Got Talent (Season 2, January 2026, Golden Buzzer at the auditions). Born in Ferrara, Italy, to Albanian parents and raised in New York, Nikolas performs in English, Italian and Albanian with his puppet sidekick Chris. He has performed on Rai 1 in Italy ("Dalla strada al palco", 2026), won 1st place at the VentHaven ConVENTion Junior Open Mic (2019) and Senior Open Mic (2021), and was featured at VentFest 2023. As an actor he is known for "The Dummy Detective" (IMDb). He is also a singer, performing live in three languages.

Dashnor Bushi, magician

New York based magician blending sleight-of-hand, mentalism and comedy. A sought-after performer for corporate and private events throughout New York City, member of the Society of American Magicians, Golden Buzzer recipient and finalist at Albania's Got Talent 2026 alongside his son.
