Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026 · Applies worldwide

Who we are

This website (www.bushishow.com) presents the shows of Nikolas and Dashnor Bushi, entertainers based in New York (USA). For anything related to your data, contact us at booking@bushishow.com.

What data we collect, and why

  • Booking form: name, email and message — used only to reply to your request. Delivered to our email inbox; never used for advertising.
  • Reviews: name, email, rating and text. The email is never published: it is used only to verify the review. The review appears on the site only after manual approval, and can be removed on request.
  • Visit counting: fully anonymous. We count daily visits as a plain number; we do not store IP addresses, names or profiles, and we cannot identify visitors.
  • Preferences: your chosen language is saved in your own browser (localStorage) so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.

Cookies

This site uses only technical cookies and local storage, strictly needed for it to work. No advertising cookies, no profiling, no tracking pixels. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data — anywhere in the world.

Third-party services we rely on, which may process technical data (such as your IP address) to deliver their function:

  • YouTube (Google): videos are embedded in "privacy-enhanced" mode (youtube-nocookie.com) — YouTube sets no tracking cookies unless you press play. If you play a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • Cloudflare: protects the site from attacks and delivers it quickly worldwide; may use strictly necessary security cookies.
  • Supabase: hosts our content database (shows, photos, reviews) on secure servers.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the site's typefaces.
  • FormSubmit: delivers the booking form messages to our inbox.

Your rights — wherever you live

Whether you are in the European Union (GDPR), the United Kingdom, Switzerland (FADP), California (CCPA/CPRA) or anywhere else: you may ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal data you have sent us (for example a booking message or a review). Write to booking@bushishow.com and we will take care of it promptly. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Data retention & transfers

Booking messages are kept only as long as needed to manage the engagement. Reviews stay online until you ask us to remove them. Data is processed on servers located in the USA and Europe, protected by the providers listed above.

Children

Our shows are family entertainment and this site is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children; the booking and review forms are intended for adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
